## Scope

**In:** the two new verbs; `rolling-no-validation` and `restart-all` served on `submitApply`, `rollbackApply`,
`restoreRuntimeConfig` and `submitFleetApply`; the server-recorded outage confirmation; undeclare (U-C); placement
retirement's `instance_retirement` record (R-1, `OPS-Q12`; the operation itself is built by plan 008 S3); the
multi-placement rules of the two ungated strategies at fleet level (plan 008 `OQ-8.1` (b)); the `scheduled` strategy
(`OPS-Q9` Sch-2, shape (c)); the Operator's `instance.stop` (`OPS-Q11` (a)); and, by `OPS-Q13` (a), the catalog rows of
plan 013's three verbs (T1).

**Out (design §1):** package actions, `group.*`, agent verbs, re-declare/archive/restore, live log-level set, metric
typing, a manual `APPLY_INDETERMINATE` resolution API, a plain restart operation, `mustBeUniformAcrossReplicas`
(routed to plan 008), scheduled fleets, the `B/D14` scheduled **workload type** (its round-5 cut is untouched).
